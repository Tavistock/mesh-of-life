Mesh of Life

Conway's Game of Life running on the faces of a 3D mesh. Every triangle is one cell, and neighbors are determined by shared edges or vertices — so Life evolves across the surface of spheres, tori, Klein bottles, the Stanford Bunny, and any model you drag in.

Getting around

  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Click or drag on the mesh to paint cells with the brush.
  • Drag & drop a .glb, .gltf, .obj, .stl, .ply, or .fbx file to load it.
  • Use the Decals panel to stamp an image or QR code onto the mesh.
